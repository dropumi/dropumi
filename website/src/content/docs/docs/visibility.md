---
title: Visibility
description: Control who can see your device and who can send to you.
---

While dropumi is receiving, other devices on the same network can see your device name. Who can actually send to you depends on the **Who can send** setting:

- **Paired only** (the default): only devices you paired by comparing a 6-digit code can send. A device you have not paired is turned away before any code is shown. Other devices can still see your device name.
- **Everyone · 10 min**: for 10 minutes, any nearby device can ask to send. For a device you have not paired, you both compare a 6-digit code, and you decide whether to accept. This lets that one transfer through; it does not pair the devices, so every transfer from that device needs its own code. After 10 minutes it goes back to Paired only.
- **Off**: dropumi is not receiving. Other devices cannot find you or send to you.

So pair once with each device you want to receive from, in [Settings](../settings/) › **Pair a device** (see [Send a file](../send-a-file/#pair-once)), and then send. A device you have not paired is never accepted automatically. You can still send files to others while receiving is off. On iPhone and iPad, dropumi receives only while it is open.

Change this in [Settings](../settings/).
