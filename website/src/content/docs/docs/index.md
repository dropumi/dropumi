dropumi is in early development. These docs describe the intended behavior and will change as the app takes shape.
:::

dropumi is a free, open-source, ultra-lightweight way to share files between your phones and computers. It works like AirDrop, but across platforms, and it needs no internet connection.

## How it works

1. Open dropumi on two devices that are on the same Wi-Fi network or hotspot.
2. Nearby devices appear on the radar.
3. Pick a file, tap a device, and the other person accepts.

Files travel directly between the two devices over your local network. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
