title: Troubleshooting
description: Fix common problems.
---

## A device does not show up

- Check that both devices are on the same Wi-Fi or local network.
- Turn off guest-network or client isolation on your router.
- On iPhone, iPad and Mac, allow local network access when asked.
- Check that the other device is receiving: **Who can send** is not Off, and on iPhone or iPad, dropumi is open.
- Allow dropumi through your firewall on desktop.
- Use **Connect by address** with the address the other device shows under **Settings**, on its device page.

## A transfer is slow

Speed depends on your Wi-Fi. Move closer to the router, or use a 5 GHz network.

## A transfer stopped on iPhone or iPad

iOS may stop a transfer when you leave dropumi. Keep it open until the transfer finishes.

## Still stuck?

[Open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/dropumi/dropumi/issues) with your devices and what you tried.
