---
title: Send a file
description: Pair once, then send files to a nearby device.
---

Open dropumi on both devices, on the same Wi-Fi or local network. By default, a device accepts files only from devices it is paired with (**Paired only**, see [Visibility](../visibility/)), so pair the two devices once before the first transfer.

## Pair once

1. On one device, go to **Settings** › **Pair a device** and tap **Wait for a device**. For five minutes, another device can ask to pair with it.
2. On the other device, go to **Settings** › **Pair a device** and tap **Pair** next to the waiting device.
3. Both screens show a 6-digit code. On each device, tap **Codes match** only if the two codes are the same.

After that, the two devices recognise each other and can send to each other without comparing a code. They are listed under **Paired devices** in [Settings](../settings/).

## Send

1. On the sender, wait for the receiving device to appear under **Nearby**. The receiver must be receiving: its **Who can send** is not **Off**, and on iPhone and iPad dropumi is open.
2. Tap **Send** next to the device. This opens the **Send** screen with that device chosen. Nothing is sent yet.
3. Add files with **Add files**, or photos with **Add photos** on iPhone and iPad. On a Mac or iPad you can also drop files onto the window. Dropping only adds the files to the list; it does not send them.
4. Check that the right device is chosen under **Send to**, then tap the **Send to** button with the device's name at the bottom of the screen.
5. The receiver sees who is sending and what. When they accept, the transfer starts.

You can send several files at once. Folders cannot be sent yet. Progress shows on the **Transfers** screen. On iPhone and iPad, keep dropumi open until the transfer finishes, or iOS may stop it.

## Send to a device you have not paired

A receiver set to **Paired only** turns away a device it is not paired with before any code is shown, and the sender sees **Only paired devices**. To take files from a device it has not paired, the receiver allows **Everyone · 10 min** (see [Visibility](../visibility/)). Then both screens show a 6-digit code: the sender taps **Codes match** and the receiver taps **Accept** only if the codes are the same.

This lets that one transfer through. It does not pair the devices: the next transfer needs a new code, and works only while the receiver still allows **Everyone**. To skip the code, [pair once](#pair-once).

## From another app (iPhone and iPad only)

On iPhone and iPad, choose dropumi in another app's share sheet. The share sheet only hands the files over: open dropumi, and they are waiting on the **Send** screen. Choose the device there and tap the **Send to** button. The Mac app has no Share Extension yet.
