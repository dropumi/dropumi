---
title: Send a file
description: Send files to a nearby device.
---

1. Open dropumi on both devices, on the same Wi-Fi or local network.
2. On the sender, wait for the receiving device to appear on the radar.
3. Tap **Send** next to the device, then add files or photos. On a Mac or iPad you can also drop files onto the window.
4. If the two devices are not paired, both screens show a 6-digit code. Go on only if the codes match. This works only while the receiver allows **Everyone** (see [Visibility](../visibility/)).
5. The receiver sees a prompt. When they accept, the transfer starts.

You can send several files at once. Folders cannot be sent yet. Progress shows on the **Transfers** screen. On iPhone and iPad, keep dropumi open until the transfer finishes, or iOS may stop it.

From another app, share files to dropumi from the share sheet, then open dropumi and choose the device there.
